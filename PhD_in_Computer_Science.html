FERNANDO LUCATELLI NUNES

Ph.D. in Mathematics     Ph.D. in Computer Science

I am a researcher in Pure Mathematics and Computer Science. My work in mathematics centers on the theory and applications of low- and higher-dimensional categorical structures, often developed from the perspective of two- and three-dimensional universal algebra. In computer science, I focus on programming language semantics and formal methods, guided by the understanding that mathematical structure can ground the design of practical and reliable tools for real-world problems.
