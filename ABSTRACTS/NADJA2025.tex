	\Title{$ N $-fold groupoids and $ n $-groupoids in regular Mal'tsev categories}
	
	\Field{Speaker}{Nadja Egner}
	\Affil{\hspace{1.15cm}Universit\'{e} catholique de Louvain, Belgium}
	
	\noindent \Field{Date \& Time}{December 02nd, 2025 (Tuesday) — 4{:}00\,PM (Coimbra time)}
	\noindent \Field{Place}{Google Meet}
	
	\Abstract
	The category $ 2\textrm{-}\mathrm{Grpd}(\mathscr{C}) $ of internal $ 2 $-groupoids in a regular Mal’tsev category $ \mathscr{C} $ with finite colimits is a Birkhoff subcategory of the category $ \mathrm{Grpd}^2(\mathscr{C}) $ of internal double groupoids in $ \mathscr{C} $, and the left adjoint of the inclusion functor of $ 2\textrm{-}\mathrm{Grpd}(\mathscr{C}) $ into $ \mathrm{Grpd}^2(\mathscr{C}) $ allows for an easy explicit descripton [4]. Using this one can show that also the category $ n\textrm{-}\mathrm{Grpd}(\mathscr{C}) $ of internal $ n $-groupoids in $ \mathscr{C} $ [1] is a Birkhoff subcategory of the category $ \mathrm{Grpd}^n(\mathscr{C}) $ of internal $ n $-fold groupoids in $ \mathscr{C} $. Since $ n\textrm{-}\mathrm{Grpd}(\mathscr{C}) $ is also coreflective in $ \mathrm{Grpd}^n(\mathscr{C}) $ (as soon as $ \mathscr{C} $ is finitely complete), the result in [4] that $ \mathrm{Grpd}^n(\mathscr{C}) $ is action representable [2] implies that also $ n\textrm{-}\mathrm{Grpd}(\mathscr{C}) $ is action representable whenever $\mathscr{C}$ is a semi-abelian algebraically coherent category with normalizers that is action representable.
