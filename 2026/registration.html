Registration information

Participation in the seminar is free. Advance registration was required for all participants, whether or not they proposed a talk.

The completed form was to be sent to Maria Manuel Clementino in the body of an email, using the following details:

To
Maria Manuel Clementino · mmc@mat.uc.pt
Subject
PCS-2026 SUBMISSION

No attachments were required for registration without a talk.

Abstract submissions

Talk proposals required an abstract prepared using the template below, with both the .tex source and the compiled .pdf attached to the registration email.

For Subject area, applicants were asked to describe the subject of their abstract in their own words; there was no fixed list of subject areas. MSC2020 classification was optional.
