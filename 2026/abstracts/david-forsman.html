XVI Portuguese Category Seminar · Abstract

On the exactness of categories of internal groups

David Forsman

Institut de Recherche en Mathématique et Physique, Université catholique de Louvain, Belgium

Friday, 13 November · 11:30–11:50Sala Pedro NunesDownload abstract (PDF)

The category Hopfkcoc\mathrm{Hopf}_k^\mathrm{coc} of cocommutative Hopf algebras over a field kk forms a semi-abelian, in particular Barr-exact, category [2]. It is the category 𝖦𝗋𝗉(𝒞)\mathsf{Grp}(\mathcal{C}) of internal groups in 𝒞=𝖢𝗈𝗆𝗈𝗇kcoc\mathcal{C}=\mathsf{Comon}^{\mathrm{coc}}_k, which is itself not exact. The argument for exactness requires a classical result by Newman [3].

Let 𝒞\mathcal{C} be a finitely complete category with reflexive coequalizers that are preserved by the binary product componentwise. For an internal group hh, let 𝒞h\mathcal{C}^h be the category of hh-actions. There is a surjective Galois connection Sub𝖦𝗋𝗉(h)⇄Quot𝒞h(h)\mathrm{Sub}_{\mathsf{Grp}}(h)\rightleftarrows \mathrm{Quot}_{\mathcal{C}^h}(h) from subgroups g≤hg\le h to regular quotients of hh in 𝒞h\mathcal{C}^h. A subgroup gg is sent to its coset quotient h→h/gh\to h/g, the coequalizer of the coset (equivalence) relation h×g⇉hh\times g\rightrightarrows h, and a regular quotient q:h→mq\colon h\to m to the stabilizer q−1(q(e))q^{-1}(q(e)) of its base point.

A subgroup g≤hg\leq h is a stabilizer precisely when its coset relation is effective. When all coset relations are effective, 𝖦𝗋𝗉(𝒞)\mathsf{Grp}(\mathcal{C}) is exact if and only if it is regular, which is equivalent to a condition on composites of regular epimorphisms. In this way, Newman’s correspondence becomes a categorical criterion for the exactness of 𝖦𝗋𝗉(𝒞)\mathsf{Grp}(\mathcal{C}). We also show that regular monomorphisms of 𝖦𝗋𝗉(𝒞)\mathsf{Grp}(\mathcal{C}) are stabilizers. If 𝒞\mathcal{C} is cartesian closed and every object embeds into an internal group, then conversely every stabilizer is regular. As a consequence, every monomorphism of cocommutative Hopf algebras is regular.

Affine group schemes also form an exact category [4], [1], but the technique does not apply as the coset relations need not be effective (the Borel subgroup of SL2\mathrm{SL}_2).

Keywords: Internal groups; Barr-exact categories; semi-abelian categories; cocommutative Hopf algebras; affine group schemes

Subject area: Theory of Categorical Structures
