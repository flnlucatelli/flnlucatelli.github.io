XVI Portuguese Category Seminar · Abstract

The center of an associative crossed module

David Quiroga Gutiérrez

Mathematics, Universidade de Santiago de Compostela, España

Joint work with Emzar Khmaladze, Manuel Ladra and Brais Ramos Pérez.

Friday, 13 November · 11:50–12:10Sala Pedro NunesDownload abstract (PDF)

Crossed modules of associative algebras are equivalent to internal categories in associative algebras, that is, to strict associative 2-algebras. For a crossed module μ:𝖬→𝖠\mu\colon\mathsf{M}\to\mathsf{A}, the multiplication of 𝖠\mathsf{A} makes the associated internal category 𝐂𝐚𝐭(𝖬,𝖠,μ)\mathbf{Cat}(\mathsf{M},\mathsf{A},\mu) a strict semimonoidal category. In general it has no unit, since our algebras need not be unital. We introduce and study a center in this setting, inspired by the Drinfeld center of a monoidal category and by M. Pirashvili’s centers of crossed modules of groups and of Lie algebras. Its objects are triples (σ,τ,a)(\sigma,\tau,a), where a∈𝖠a\in\mathsf{A} and σ:𝟎⇒a⋅(−)\sigma\colon\mathbf{0}\Rightarrow a\cdot(-), τ:𝟎⇒(−)⋅a\tau\colon\mathbf{0}\Rightarrow(-)\cdot a are natural transformations from the zero endofunctor that are compatible with the semimonoidal structure. In other words, each object aa comes with coherent null-homotopies of its left and right multiplication functors. The difference σ−τ:(−)⋅a⇒a⋅(−)\sigma-\tau\colon(-)\cdot a\Rightarrow a\cdot(-) plays the role of a half-braiding, but the pair (σ,τ)(\sigma,\tau) retains more information, so the construction is a Drinfeld-type center rather than the Drinfeld center itself. We prove that this center is canonically isomorphic to the internal category of an explicit crossed module 𝐙(𝖬,𝖠,μ)\mathbf{Z}(\mathsf{M},\mathsf{A},\mu), built from Mac Lane’s bimultiplications. We also show that it carries a natural braiding satisfying the strict hexagon identities. Hence the center is a braided semimonoidal category, corresponding to a braided crossed module. It fits into a crossed square and into a homotopy fibre sequence that relates its homotopy invariants π0\pi_0 and π1\pi_1 to those of (𝖬,𝖠,μ)(\mathsf{M},\mathsf{A},\mu). Associative crossed modules form a semi-abelian category, so Huq’s categorical notion of center is also available. We compute the Huq center explicitly. We then show that it embeds into our center through a morphism that is an isomorphism on π1\pi_1 and injective on π0\pi_0, but is not a weak equivalence in general. Finally, we compare our center with Pirashvili’s center of Lie crossed modules via the (braided) Liezation functors. We obtain a canonical comparison morphism, which preserves braidings under an injectivity condition.

Keywords: Category theory; Crossed modules; Center

Subject area: Crossed modules and category theory
