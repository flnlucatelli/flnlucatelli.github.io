XVI Portuguese Category Seminar · Abstract

Ternary semi-direct products in algebraically coherent categories

Arnaud Duvieusart

IRMP, UCLouvain - FNRS, Belgium

Saturday, 14 November · 11:30–12:00Sala Pedro NunesDownload abstract (PDF)

In the category of groups, an action of BB on AA can be defined equivalently as a function B×A→AB\times A\to A (satisfying certain axioms) or as a morphism B→Aut(A)B\to \operatorname{Aut}(A). In particular, an action of a coproduct B+CB+C or a semi-direct product B⋊CB\rtimes C on AA can easily be described by a pair of actions of BB and CC on AA, corresponding to the induced morphisms from BB and CC to [A][A].

In a semi-abelian category 𝒞\mathcal{C}, an action of BB on AA is defined as a morphism ξAB:B♭A→A\xi^{B}_{A}\colon B\flat A\to A in 𝒞\mathcal{C} [1]; but a correspondence with morphisms B→[A]B\to [A] is only possible for action representable categories [2]. As a consequence, for a pair of actions (ξAB,ξAC)(\xi^{B}_{A},\xi^{C}_{A}) in 𝒞\mathcal{C}, one can guarantee neither the existence nor the uniqueness of a corresponding action ξAB+C\xi^{B+C}_A or ξAB⋊C\xi^{B\rtimes C}_A.

In this talk, we will show that, in an algebraically coherent semi-abelian category [5], an action of B⋊CB\rtimes C on AA is uniquely determined by a pair of actions (ξAB,ξAC)(\xi^{B}_{A},\xi^{C}_{A}) satisfying certain conditions. More generally, we show how ternary semi-direct products of A,B,CA,B,C, which were previously defined by Carrasco and Cegarra in the case of groups [3] and Lie algebras [4], can be described using actions ξAB\xi^{B}_{A}, ξAC\xi^{C}_{A}, ξBC\xi^{C}_{B} and an equivariant morphism φ:K[ξBC]→A\varphi\colon K[\xi^{C}_{B}]\to A.

Keywords: Semi-abelian categories; actions; semi-direct products

Subject area: Categorical Algebra

MSC2020: 18E13
