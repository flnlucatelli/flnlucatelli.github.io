XVI Portuguese Category Seminar · Abstract

Left Kan extensions and monotone-light factorizations

João Xarez

Department of Mathematics, University of Aveiro, Portugal

Friday, 13 November · 15:30–16:00Sala Pedro NunesDownload abstract (PDF)

Let 𝑆𝑒𝑡K⊢𝐿𝑎𝑛K:𝑆𝑒𝑡𝔹→𝑆𝑒𝑡𝔸\mathit{Set}^{K}\vdash \mathit{Lan}_{K}\colon \mathit{Set}^{\mathbb{B}}\to\mathit{Set}^{\mathbb{A}} be an adjunction given by left Kan extensions, and such that, for every object A∈𝔸A\in\mathbb{A}, each comma category (K↓A)(K\downarrow A) is a pseudo-filtered category, and all the morphisms in 𝔹\mathbb{B} are monomorphisms. The induced reflection from the adjunction above is “very-well-behaved”, providing an associated monotone-light factorization system on 𝑆𝑒𝑡𝔹\mathit{Set}^{\mathbb{B}}. The induced reflection I⊢HI\vdash H of 𝑆𝑒𝑡𝔹\mathit{Set}^{\mathbb{B}} into a full subcategory 𝑀𝑜𝑛𝑜(𝐿𝑎𝑛K)\mathit{Mono}(\mathit{Lan}_{K}) is obtained by factorizing (componentwise) the unit morphisms of the given adjunction through surjections and injections. This new result just stated generalizes section 3 in [3]. In fact, if 𝔹\mathbb{B} is pseudo-filtered and 𝔸=∐i∈I𝟏\mathbb{A}=\coprod_{i\in I}\mathbf{1} is the coproduct of a family of categories with just one object and one morphism (ordinal number one), it can be shown that this is a special case of the more general one above. In particular, for 𝔸=𝟏\mathbb{A}=\mathbf{1} the left adjoint 𝐿𝑎𝑛K\mathit{Lan}_{K} becomes the colimit functor, and in the very simple case with 𝔹=𝟐\mathbb{B}=\mathbf{2} (ordinal number two) the monotone-light factorization is non-trivial (that is, distinct from the reflective factorization system), as shown in section 3 in [3]; notice that, for instance, if 𝔹=𝟐\mathbb{B}=\mathbf{2} and 𝔸=𝟏⊔𝟏\mathbb{A}=\mathbf{1}\sqcup\mathbf{1} then the monotone-light factorization is the same as when 𝔸=𝟏\mathbb{A}=\mathbf{1}.

Keywords: Monotone-light factorization; pseudo-filtered category; left Kan extensions; presheaves

Subject area: Category theory

MSC2020: 18A40, 18A32, 18E50
