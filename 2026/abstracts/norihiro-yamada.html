XVI Portuguese Category Seminar · Abstract

On Graphical Freeness for Graded Linear Categories

Norihiro Yamada

Centre for Mathematics, University of Coimbra, Portugal

Saturday, 14 November · 09:30–10:00Sala Pedro NunesDownload abstract (PDF)

In this talk, I sketch a quotient-free graphical calculus for a free graded linear category over an arbitrary category, together with its application to a coherence problem (if time permits).

Linear categories [1] elegantly axiomatise a canonical interplay between linear and nonlinear maps recurring across logic and mathematics. Despite the intricate interaction of the comonad with the symmetric monoidal and the cartesian structures of a linear category (e.g. its standard formal theory comes with seventy knotty formal equations), I have established a graphical calculus for a free linear category over an arbitrary category [2]. To my knowledge, it is the first quotient-free presentation of the free linear category that reduces algebra to geometry: two morphisms are equal if and only if their graphical representatives are the same graph.

On the one hand, the graphical calculus induces quantitative information through an interpretation of paths as resource-sensitive computations. On the other hand, linear categories graded by semiring elements [3] refine linear categories, making the quantitative intuition behind them explicit. I then conjectured that the two quantitative structures coincide: the graphical calculus forms a free graded linear category over the base category. A positive answer to this question justifies graded linear categories as a canonical quantitative refinement of linear categories. I have obtained a proof sketch that solves the conjecture in the positive. In this talk, I introduce the graphical calculus, outline the proof, and, should time allow, state a necessary, sufficient graphical condition for coherence on graded linear categories.

Keywords: graded linear categories; graphical calculi; coherence

Subject area: graphical calculi

MSC2020: 18A50
