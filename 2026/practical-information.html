Planning your visit

Practical Information

Getting to Coimbra, finding the seminar, and making the most of your stay.

Arriving by air? Fly to Porto Airport (OPO) or Lisbon Airport (LIS), then continue to Coimbra by train, intercity coach or a pre-booked airport transfer. The train routes require a Metro or taxi connection from the airport to a railway station; direct airport coaches are also available.

The train times below cover the rail journey only. Allow additional time for your flight arrival, airport transfer and connection. Use the linked operators to check departures and fares for your travel date.

By train

Book long-distance trains to Coimbra-B. CP operates Alfa Pendular and Intercidades services from Porto-Campanhã and Lisboa-Oriente; tickets for these trains include a reserved seat. From Coimbra-B, continue by local transport or taxi to your hotel or the venue.

From Porto Airport

OPO airport → Porto-Campanhã → Coimbra-B

  1. Reach Campanhã. Take Metro line E (purple) towards central Porto. If your service terminates at Trindade, change there to a train serving Campanhã. At Campanhã, follow the signs to the CP railway station. A taxi or ride-hailing trip is another option for this airport connection.
  2. Take the train to Coimbra-B. Choose an Alfa Pendular or Intercidades service. The rail journey takes approximately 1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes, depending on the service.

Porto Metro maps & timetables · CP train tickets

From Lisbon Airport

LIS airport → Lisboa-Oriente → Coimbra-B

  1. Reach Oriente. Take the Metro red line from Aeroporto towards São Sebastião and leave at Oriente. The Metro journey is approximately 10 minutes. Follow the signs to the railway station. You can also take a taxi or request a ride from the airport.
  2. Take the train to Coimbra-B. Choose an Alfa Pendular or Intercidades service. The rail journey takes approximately 1 hour 35 minutes to 2 hours, depending on the service.

Lisbon Airport transport · CP train tickets

Check the CP intercity timetable and leave a comfortable connection after your flight.

By coachIntercity buses

FlixBus and Rede Expressos provide intercity coach options. Compare the exact departure and arrival stops as well as the timetable: airport services and city-terminal services board at different places.

From Porto

Directly from the airport. FlixBus lists Porto Airport–Coimbra services. The airport boarding area is P8 bus parking. Select your date and follow the precise boarding location on your ticket.

From the city. You can also compare departures from Porto’s Campanhã coach terminal. From the airport, take the Metro towards Campanhã as described above, then follow signs to the coach terminal. The coach terminal and CP railway station are separate facilities.

From Lisbon

Directly from the airport. FlixBus lists Lisbon Airport–Coimbra services. The airport stop is the BUS PARK on Alameda das Comunidades Portuguesas. Check the departure time and location on your ticket.

From the city. Take the airport Metro red line to Oriente and choose a coach departing from Lisboa Oriente. When comparing services, check the terminal carefully: a departure from Sete Rios uses a different terminal and requires a different airport connection.

Where will your coach arrive? FlixBus currently lists its Coimbra stop at Rua do Padrão, beneath the road bridge. Other operators may use different stops. Use the arrival location on your ticket when planning your onward journey; do not assume the coach arrives at Coimbra-B railway station.

Airport transfers

Coimbra Airport Shuttle offers pre-booked transfers between Coimbra and both Porto and Lisbon airports. This is an option if you prefer fewer changes with luggage.

Check the available departure times, pickup arrangements, Coimbra drop-off address and total fare before booking. Your hotel may also be able to help arrange a private airport transfer.

Reaching the venue

The seminar is hosted by the Centre for Mathematics of the University of Coimbra (CMUC), in the Department of Mathematics on the historic Alta / Pólo I campus.

From Coimbra-B or your coach stop

For a straightforward arrival with luggage, take a taxi or request an Uber or Bolt to “Departamento de Matemática, Largo D. Dinis”. Use this destination rather than a generic University pin, as the University has several campuses.

From Coimbra-B by public transport. Take a Metrobus displaying República to Praça da República. From there, choose an SMTUC 103 departure that serves Universidade, then walk to Largo D. Dinis; alternatively, walk uphill from Praça da República. Some 103 services start or finish at Praça da República, so check the destination and the timetable for your day of travel.

Metro Mondego routes & timetables · Bus 103 timetable

On foot

The Department is at Largo D. Dinis, near the top of the Escadas Monumentais (Monumental Stairs). You can walk up from Praça da República or the historic centre, but Coimbra is hilly and some routes include long flights of steps. A taxi or local bus is more comfortable with luggage or if steep climbs are difficult.

For help with step-free access to the seminar, please contact the organisers in advance. Check the programme for room details when announced.

Local transport

By city bus · SMTUC

SMTUC operates Coimbra’s city buses. For the university connection from Praça da República, check line 103 and choose a journey serving Universidade. Some departures finish at Praça da República.

The seminar takes place on Thursday, Friday and Saturday: consult the relevant weekday or Saturday timetable and departure point. You can buy an onboard ticket from the driver if you do not already have a valid ticket. If using a travel card, validate it whenever you board; see the SMTUC ticket guidance.

All SMTUC routes · Line 103 timetable

By Metrobus · Metro Mondego

Metro Mondego provides the Metrobus service, including a connection between Coimbra-B and República. Check the destination display before boarding: services on the urban network have different termini.

República is the interchange for the onward university bus or an uphill walk to Largo D. Dinis. Buy or load a ticket at the station machines and validate your card on the platform before boarding; Metrobus vehicles have no onboard validators. See the current timetable and ticket information.

By taxi

Use a taxi rank, ask your hotel to book, or call Politáxis Coimbra on +351 239 499 090. For the seminar, give the destination “Departamento de Matemática, Largo D. Dinis”.

By Uber or Bolt

Uber and Bolt serve Coimbra. Use the app to check availability, the fare estimate and the pickup point, particularly around stations and pedestrian streets. Set the venue destination to the Department of Mathematics at Largo D. Dinis.

On foot

The Alta, the Botanical Garden and the riverfront are rewarding places to explore on foot. Allow for the climb back to the University and for steps on some routes. Comfortable shoes and a rain layer are useful for a November visit.

Use current timetables. The urban network was updated on 10 September 2026, including Coimbra-B and República connections and changes to bus 103. The municipality’s network update explains the changes.

Beyond the seminar

Explore Coimbra

From the hilltop University to the lanes of the old town and the banks of the Mondego, there is plenty to discover on foot. Choose a short walk between sessions, a museum visit or an evening of Coimbra fado.

At the Paço das Escolas

The Joanina Library

The Biblioteca Joanina is one of Coimbra’s great highlights. Completed in 1728, this Baroque library has richly gilded bookshelves, painted ceilings and a historic collection of around 60,000 volumes. Its location in the Paço das Escolas makes it a natural visit while exploring the university quarter.

  • Choose a ticket that includes the library. The University offers several visiting programmes; select one explicitly including the Joanina Library, such as Joanina Library + University + Science Museum.
  • Book a timed entry. Your ticket fixes the date and time of entry to the library building, through the Academic Prison. Reserve in advance and arrive in time for your slot; the other spaces follow the conditions of your chosen ticket.
  • During the visit. Photography and filming are prohibited on the Noble Floor. For wheelchair access, contact the tourism staff in advance; access is restricted to the Noble Floor.

Near the venue

The Botanical Garden

The University’s Botanical Garden offers a quiet walk among trees, terraces and historic garden architecture. The entrance by the Arcos do Jardim is a convenient starting point from the university area.

The published winter opening hours are 09:00–17:30, from October to March. Individual informal visits are free. Check the official visitor information for opening exceptions and access to particular spaces; restoration works currently restrict some areas.

The University & the Alta

Walk through the historic university quarter and the Paço das Escolas, and enjoy the views over Coimbra. The Royal Palace and Saint Michael’s Chapel can be visited through the University’s ticketed programmes; check access and opening times for your day of visit.

The Mondego riverfront

Leave time for a stroll beside the river and a view back towards the University from the Santa Clara side. The riverfront offers a gentler walk after the hills of the Alta.

The old town & Santa Cruz

From the Alta, explore the lanes around the Sé Velha, Coimbra’s Romanesque cathedral. Continue downhill through the Quebra Costas and the Almedina arch into the Baixa, with its shops, squares and cafés.

At Praça 8 de Maio, visit the Monastery of Santa Cruz, which houses the tombs of Portugal’s first two kings. This makes a natural end to a walk from the University.

Machado de Castro Museum

Close to the University, the Museu Nacional de Machado de Castro brings together sculpture, painting and decorative arts above the remarkable Roman cryptoporticus: the underground galleries that supported the forum of ancient Aeminium.

Check access before visiting. Restoration works and storm damage have affected access in 2026. Confirm which galleries and whether the cryptoporticus are open before planning a visit; a full reopening has not been verified. See the municipal announcement about the works and the museum’s current visitor information.

Across the river: Santa Clara

Cross Ponte de Santa Clara for views of the old city from the opposite bank. Nearby Santa Clara-a-Velha, the medieval monastery shaped by the Mondego’s floods, is temporarily closed for works according to its official ticket page at the time of this review. Check the current access notice before planning an interior visit.

For a longer outing, consider the gardens of Quinta das Lágrimas, associated with the story of Pedro and Inês. The gardens have their own admission arrangements; check their opening times separately.

An evening of Coimbra fado

Coimbra has its own tradition of song and Portuguese guitar, closely associated with university life. A live performance is a pleasant way to round off an evening in the old town.

Fado ao Centro, on Rua do Quebra Costas, is one option; consult its current programme and book directly. During the day, stop at a café to try an arrufada or a pastel de Santa Clara.

A short city walk. Start at the Paço das Escolas, continue to the Sé Velha, descend through the Quebra Costas and Almedina, and finish at Santa Cruz in the Baixa. Set aside around two hours for an unhurried stroll and a café stop, with extra time for ticketed visits.

A gentler option: the Mondego riverfront. Descend towards Portagem and follow the river through Jardim Manuel Braga and Parque Verde. Make this an out-and-back walk of whatever length suits you. The riverside paths are gentler than the climb back to the University; a taxi can spare the return ascent.

River crossing. Works on the Pedro e Inês footbridge were scheduled from 6 October for up to three weeks, subject to weather. Check reopening before relying on that crossing; the municipality recommends Ponte de Santa Clara during the closure.

Gardens, woodland & river valleys

Nature walks in and around Coimbra

For a quiet break in the city, try Penedo da Saudade or the Mondego riverfront. With more time, explore Vale de Canas, the wooded valleys of Buçaco or the rock formations along the Mondego and Ribeira de Alge. These are suggestions for independent outings before or after the seminar.

Within Coimbra · A short garden walk

Penedo da Saudade

A leafy garden and viewpoint with stone poetry plaques and views across eastern Coimbra. It is a pleasant place to sit or stroll without arranging a trip out of town.

From the Department. Walk towards Arcos do Jardim and continue to the garden entrance on Avenida Marnoco e Sousa. Allow for slopes along the approach and steps within the garden. For a flatter walk, choose the Mondego riverfront.

Municipal garden information

Coimbra’s outskirts · A short woodland outing

Mata Nacional de Vale de Canas

PR1 CBR · 1.1 km circuit · Published walking time: 45 minutes · 29 m ascent

The interpretive trail passes native and exotic trees, an aromatic garden and a viewpoint over the forest. The official route has a low effort rating, although the woodland surface is uneven; wear shoes with grip.

Getting there. This forest is outside the central walking area. A taxi to the entrance, with a return pickup arranged, is a straightforward option. The official route page includes a map; check local access notices before setting out.

Official trail description & map

Penacova · A half-day outing

Livraria do Mondego

Interpretive trail · 0.8 km linear · Published walking time: 30 minutes · Easy

Follow the river beside striking upright quartzite formations, named for their resemblance to books on a shelf. This short trail on the Mondego’s left bank is a good choice for river and rock scenery without a long hike.

Plan the return. The distance and time above are for the linear trail; allow extra time to retrace your steps. Use the short interpretive route shown in the official map. A car or pre-arranged taxi to the trailhead, with a return pickup, makes this easiest to combine with a visit to Penacova.

Official trail description & map

Figueiró dos Vinhos · A half-day outing

Fragas de São Simão & Casal de São Simão

Signed route · About 1.7 km linear · Published walking time: 45 minutes · Moderate

A wooded gorge, rocky viewpoints and the small village of Casal de São Simão make this a varied outing. The route links the viewpoint above the Fragas with the village, descending to the Ribeira de Alge and its river beach before climbing again.

Plan the return. Allow additional time for the return walk and stops; despite its short distance, this is a moderate route with descents and climbs. Travel by car or arrange a taxi and return pickup. Bring water and a snack rather than relying on seasonal facilities by the river.

Official route leaflet & map (PDF) · Destination website & visitor information

Walking in November. Start early, leave enough daylight for the return and carry a rain layer, water and shoes with grip. The published walking times exclude travel from Coimbra and extra stops. Check the weather forecast and the linked local access information shortly before travelling; follow marked routes and any closure signs. Postpone riverside, gorge or steep woodland walks after heavy rain or during strong winds.

For a longer forest walk. See Buçaco’s Fern Valley and the 6.7 km Trees Trail below. For an outing by public transport, the Figueira da Foz seafront is another option.

A little further afield

Short excursions from Coimbra

The Atlantic coast, Roman ruins, woodland walks and canal-side towns are all options for extending your stay. These are ideas for independent outings before or after the seminar; the suggested half-day and full-day plans include time to explore.

Atlantic coast · Half-day or full day

Figueira da Foz & Buarcos

For a trip to the beach, head to Figueira da Foz: broad sands, Atlantic views and a long seafront make it a rewarding change of scene. Continue towards Buarcos for a coastal walk and lunch. In November, plan around a walk by the sea and time in town.

Getting there. CP runs trains from Coimbra-B to Figueira da Foz. Allow for the journey from the station to the seafront, and check the return train before setting off. A car gives more flexibility if you want to explore further along the coast.

Roman archaeology · Half-day

Conímbriga

Explore the remains of a Roman town, with elaborate mosaic floors, baths and the House of Fountains. Leave time for the museum as well as the archaeological site; much of the visit is outdoors.

Getting there. The museum’s visitor page lists SIT-RC buses from Coimbra, as well as access by car or taxi via Condeixa-a-Nova. Check the exact stop, outward journey and return service for your chosen day; if taking a taxi, arrange the return pickup.

Woodland & gardens · Half-day or full day

Buçaco forest & Luso

Walk among the mature trees of the Mata Nacional do Buçaco, seek out the Fern Valley and Fonte Fria, and admire the Palace Hotel’s exterior. Combine the forest with a stroll through the nearby spa town of Luso, or leave more time for the woodland paths.

Choose your walk. For a shorter visit, explore Vale dos Fetos (Fern Valley) and Fonte Fria using the forest map. For a longer walk, the Trilho das Árvores Notáveis is a 6.7 km circuit with a published walking time of 2 hours 25 minutes; allow longer for stops and expect climbs. It takes in the forest’s remarkable trees, Fern Valley and historic features.

Getting there. A car or a pre-arranged taxi makes it easier to combine the forest and Luso. For public transport, consult CP for Luso-Buçaco and plan the onward journey to the forest: the railway stop is not at the Palace Hotel. Check the forest foundation’s access information before travelling.

November hours. The foundation lists gate/toll services from 09:00 to 17:00 in November–February. Plan a daytime visit and consult the current hours and route notices before departure.

Canals & coast · Full day

Aveiro & Costa Nova

Explore Aveiro’s canals and Art Nouveau buildings, and try the local ovos moles. With a full day, continue to Costa Nova for its colourful striped houses, lagoon-side views and Atlantic beach. If you prefer a shorter outing, spend the time in Aveiro itself.

Getting there. Take a CP train from Coimbra-B to Aveiro. Costa Nova requires a separate onward journey: check BusWay route 36, Aveiro–Costa Nova, or take a taxi. Plan the return bus and train together, using the timetable for your travel date.

Explore Aveiro · Costa Nova · CP trains · BusWay routes & timetables

Planning for November. Make the most of daylight for the coast and woodland walks, and bring a rain layer and comfortable shoes. Choose a museum or an old-town visit if the weather is unsettled. Check opening days and return transport in advance, especially for Sunday outings after the seminar.
