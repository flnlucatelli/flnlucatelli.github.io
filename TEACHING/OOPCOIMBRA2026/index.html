Working with the examples

I recommend reading the explanation, predicting the result and then running the complete program. Change one part at a time and explain why the result changes. Each example is independent; copy or compile one complete file at a time.

The interactive pages connect each slide with its source code. Exercises include an initial program and a separate solution to consult after attempting the problem.

Individual projects

The individual projects are organised into ten tasks, with supporting material for each stage. I expect regular progress, recorded through one or two meaningful commits each week and pushed to the corresponding repository.

The course test will include questions about the individual projects, as well as general questions on object-oriented programming. Be prepared to explain your choices, follow the execution of your program and relate its implementation to the concepts discussed in class.

For the tasks assigned to your project and their deadlines, consult your repository and the course announcements.
