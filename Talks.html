FERNANDO LUCATELLI NUNES

Ph.D. in Mathematics     Ph.D. in Computer Science

My research advances the theory and applications of low- and higher-dimensional generalised categorical structures, organised through two- and three-dimensional universal algebra as a unifying framework.

In computer science, I specialise in programming language semantics and formal methods, guided by the view that mathematical structure can provide a principled foundation for the design of practical, reliable, and robust tools for real-world systems.
