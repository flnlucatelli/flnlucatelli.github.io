FERNANDO LUCATELLI NUNES

Ph.D. in Mathematics

 

My research interests and contributions have revolved around the theory and applications of low and higher dimensional categorical structures, mostly with a viewpoint from two and three-dimensional universal algebra.

Currently, I am studying Computer Sciences at Utrecht University, under the supervision of Dr. Matthijs Vákár. My main interests in Computer Sciences have revolved around Programming Languages.
