Research leadership

Projects Led as Principal Investigator

This section comprises my independently directed research programme and the fellowship-supported projects for which I served as principal investigator.

  • Freely generated categorical structures for programming language semantics

    Current research programme. Research leadership: Fernando Lucatelli Nunes.

    • Foundations: programming languages, considered up to their equational theories, as freely generated or presented categorical structures grounded in two-dimensional universal algebra.
    • Applications: categorical semantics and correct-by-construction transformations for advanced type systems, recursion, computational effects, differentiable programming, probabilistic programming, verification, program analysis, and compiler transformations, including defunctionalisation and closure conversion.

    category theoryprogramming languagesformal methodsverified transformations

  • Grothendieck descent theory, fibrations, and factorizations

    Fields Research Fellowship, Fields Institute for Research in Mathematical Sciences, Canada, 2023. Role: Fellowship holder and Principal Investigator.

    • Collaboration: Walter Tholen.
    • Focus: the passage from Grothendieck cofibrations to factorization systems, formulated through two-dimensional monad theory.

    pure mathematicsdescent theoryfibrationsfactorizations

  • Grothendieck descent, Galois theory, and presentations of categorical structures

    Oberwolfach Leibniz Fellowship, Mathematisches Forschungsinstitut Oberwolfach, Germany, 2022. Role: Fellowship holder and Principal Investigator.

    • Collaborators: Maria Manuel Clementino, Rui Prezado, Lurdes Sousa, and Matthijs Vákár.
    • Focus: effective descent, categorical Galois theory, and presentations by coinserters and computads.

    pure mathematicsGalois theorypresentationscategorical structures
