FERNANDO LUCATELLI NUNES

Ph.D. in Mathematics

 

My research interests and contributions in Pure Mathematics have revolved around the theory and applications of low and higher dimensional categorical structures, mostly with a viewpoint from two and three-dimensional universal algebra.

Currently, I work at the Department of Information and Computing Sciences Software Technology Group at Utrecht University.
