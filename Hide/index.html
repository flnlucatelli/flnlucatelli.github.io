FERNANDO LUCATELLI NUNES

Assistant Professor, Department of Mathematics, University of Coimbra
Ph.D. in Mathematics     Ph.D. in Computer Science

I work in pure mathematics and computer science, with a focus on the theory and applications of low- and higher-dimensional generalized categorical structures. My research is driven both by foundational questions in category theory and by problems arising in programming language semantics, formal methods, and the design and implementation of modern programming languages.
