FERNANDO LUCATELLI NUNES

Ph.D. in Mathematics     Ph.D. in Computer Science

My mathematical work focuses on the theory and applications of low- and higher-dimensional (generalized) categorical structures, often approached through the lens of two- and three-dimensional universal algebra.

In Computer Science, I specialize in programming language semantics and formal methods, guided by the understanding that mathematical structure can underpin the design of practical and reliable tools for real-world problems.

I am particularly drawn to cross-disciplinary ideas and to maintaining a meaningful balance between theoretical depth and practical impact. I have a strong interest in areas of pure mathematics and theoretical computer science motivated by real-world challenges, as well as in applying rigorous theoretical frameworks to concrete problems in computer science and software engineering. For me, putting theory into practice is the most reliable way to shape principled, dependable solutions with lasting relevance.

I am always open to conversations and collaborations, especially with prospective PhD students and researchers who share an interest in categorical structures, formal methods, programming language theory, or any theme that resonates with my interests. If any of this speaks to you, feel free to get in touch.
