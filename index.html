Fernando Lucatelli Nunes

Ph.D. in Mathematics · Ph.D. in Computer Science

I work in pure mathematics and computer science, focusing on the theory and applications of low- and higher-dimensional generalized categorical structures. My research is motivated both by foundational questions in category theory and by problems in computer science, especially those arising in programming language semantics, formal methods, and the design and implementation of modern programming languages.
