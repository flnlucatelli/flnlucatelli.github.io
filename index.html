Fernando Lucatelli Nunes

Ph.D. in Mathematics · Ph.D. in Computer Science — defence September 2026

I am a mathematician and computer scientist working across category theory, programming-language semantics, and formal methods. My research develops low- and higher-dimensional categorical structures and uses them to obtain principled accounts of program transformations, automatic differentiation, recursion and iteration, algebraic effects, and probabilistic computation.
