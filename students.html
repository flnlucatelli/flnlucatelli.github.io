Research community and doctoral development

I organise the CLASS Seminar (Category, Logic, Algebra & Semantics Seminar), an international group centred on my students and open to interested PhD students elsewhere. It brings together pure mathematics and computer science, with an emphasis on sharing ideas, learning from one another, and communicating across different research backgrounds.
