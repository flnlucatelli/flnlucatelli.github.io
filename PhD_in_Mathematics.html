Pseudomonads and Descent

Joint Ph.D. Programme in Mathematics, Portugal. Awarded in 2018, summa cum laude.

  • Period October 2012 – January 2018
  • Supervisor Professor Maria Manuel Clementino
  • Committee Martin Hyland, Marco Mackaay, Lurdes Sousa, and Peter Beier Gothen.
  • Links Thesis material on arXiv; TID Portugal: 101570422.

Main themes

Pseudomonads; Grothendieck descent theory; biadjoint triangle theorems; lax algebras; pseudo-Kan extensions; freely generated categorical structures; presentations of low-dimensional categories; coherence; two-dimensional category theory.
